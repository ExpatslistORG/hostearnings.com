# HostEarnings

[HostEarnings](https://hostearnings.com) is bookkeeping and accounting software built, or custom built, for property-based businesses. It targets short-term rental operators running listings on Airbnb, VRBO, and Booking.com, along with real estate agencies, hotel businesses, and multi-property portfolio managers who need clean, audit-ready books without hiring a full accounting team. Plans live at [/en/plans](https://hostearnings.com/en/plans); feature highlights are at [/en/benefits](https://hostearnings.com/en/benefits).

## Bookkeeping and revenue reconciliation

The platform reconciles revenue across booking platforms so a host or property manager sees real income after fees, cleaning charges, and taxes, rather than the raw payout number a platform shows. Books are produced to GAAP and IFRS compliant standards, which matters for owners who need audit-ready financials or are raising capital.

## ADR, occupancy, and RevPAR analytics

Short-term rental performance metrics, including Average Daily Rate (ADR), occupancy rate, and RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room), show up on live dashboards. This lets a portfolio manager compare performance across properties without exporting spreadsheets from each booking platform separately.

## Payout imports and income statements

Hosts import payouts directly via CSV from Airbnb, VRBO, and Booking.com, and the software turns them into standard income statements. Reports can be shared as links with expiration controls, useful for sending a snapshot to an accountant, co-host, or investor without granting full account access.

## API and AI assistant access

An API, documented at [/en/api](https://hostearnings.com/en/api), plus MCP (Model Context Protocol) integration, lets AI assistants pull financial data programmatically. Security details, including encrypted data storage, are at [/en/security](https://hostearnings.com/en/security).

## Professional services

Hosts and agencies needing custom integrations or bespoke reporting beyond the standard plans can use Professional Services at [/en/services](https://hostearnings.com/en/services). A case study is published at [/en/case-studies/blissful-escape](https://hostearnings.com/en/case-studies/blissful-escape).

## Plans

A Starter tier covers up to 3 properties and a Pro tier up to 15, plus custom Professional Services for larger portfolios. Full pricing is at [/en/plans](https://hostearnings.com/en/plans); support is at [/en/contact](https://hostearnings.com/en/contact).
